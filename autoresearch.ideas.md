## Open creative space
- Glasgow tenements as isometric buildings you can climb
- Rain as a constant atmospheric presence (glyphs falling)
- Streetlights casting pools of warm light in the dark
- Cat behaviours: jump on walls, knock bins over, hiss at foxes
- Other cats, foxes, seagulls as NPCs
- Day/night cycle weighted heavily toward night
- Chip shop, off-licence, close mouths as landmarks
- Puddles reflecting streetlight
- The cat sprite itself — small, expressive, directional
