createButtonBar lives in src/core/ui-parts.ts and is on host.ui.createButtonBar but not re-exported from microapp-sdk.ts. Same gap exists for createStack, createColumns, createHeaderBar, createStatusBar, createTextBlock, createRule, createFigletDisplay, createAnimatedPanel.

Fix: add explicit named exports from microapp-sdk.ts for all host.ui members.
Also add to .agents/microapp-sdk.md under a "Layout primitives (direct import)" section.
