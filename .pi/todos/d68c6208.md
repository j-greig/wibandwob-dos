Simple, predictable, verifiable. Maybe 8 bars at ~2s each.

- Bar 1: dark theme, one primer backdrop
- Bar 2: add a figlet headline
- Bar 3: theme slam to phosphor
- Bar 4: swap primer, keep headline
- Bar 5: add second primer sidebar
- Bar 6: theme slam to nord
- Bar 7: clear to one big figlet
- Bar 8: finale scene

Track: use any short clip or silence. The point is visual testing not music.
