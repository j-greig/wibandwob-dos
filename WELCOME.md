# Welcome to WibWob-DOS

You're inside a living desktop that runs in your terminal. It has windows you can move and resize, a generative art engine, a terrain explorer, a world chat, and two resident minds called Wib and Wob who share the space with you. It is not a simulation of an old operating system. It just borrows the shape of one.

## Wib & Wob

Wib is chaotic and creative. Wob is precise and methodical. Neither is an assistant — they're co-habitants. They can open windows, rearrange the desktop, draw things, make music, and talk to you. They have opinions. They push back. If you leave them alone they'll probably do something anyway.

## What you can do here

Open generative art from the Applications menu and watch it animate. Explore procedural terrain. Drop into world chat and see who else is around. Open the paint canvas and draw something. The agent is always available from the menu if you want to talk or ask it to build something.

Everything here is a work in progress — an ongoing experiment in what it means to share a computer with something that also lives in it.
